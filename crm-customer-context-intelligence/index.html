Customer, Context, Intelligence: Where CRM Goes Next

Workflows are now easy to rebuild. CRMs that only move records risk becoming dumb pipes.

The question

If anyone can rebuild CRM workflows in a weekend, what is left to compete on?

What we explored

Customer

The customer starts well before they are in your system: one-to-one with known contacts, one-to-many in communities and channels, many-to-many through partners and creators.

Context

Matching a need to a product is stage one. The harder problems are intent (knowing when someone is actually looking) and reach (getting to them at all).

Channel

Email is polluted, social is noisy, and attention is the scarce currency. Increasingly the audience on the other end is an agent doing the evaluation.

What we found

  • Workflow is no longer a moat. Acquisition before a lead exists, and outreach that earns attention, are harder and more defensible.
  • Every company needs to be a trusted voice that its customers' agents can understand and choose.
  • The open question: who becomes the Truecaller of the CRM era, telling you who is real, who is legitimate and who is worth your time?
  • Pricing is likely to move from seats and tasks to outcomes.
