Voice rail for pre-owned luxury

Browse 120 real pre-owned jackets by talking. Ten taps of filtering become one sentence.

The question

Pre-owned luxury is browse-heavy and filter-heavy. Can voice replace faceted filtering, and keep the screen for looking at the product instead of the controls?

What we explored

The experience

Three jackets lead and ten wait quietly behind them. Say “next” to move on, or refine without starting over: “only black”, “under 400”, “cheaper first”. Every active filter shows as a hang-tag chip.

One tool registry

Nine tools cover the whole experience. Voice (Gemini Live with function calling), typing and browser agents (through WebMCP) all call the same registry, so they change the screen in exactly the same way.

Real catalogue

120 real, unsold jackets, 60 men's and 60 women's, captured from Vestiaire Collective's public search on 12 November 2026 and embedded in the page.

What we found

  • Seven spoken turns take a shopper from 120 jackets to one €158 leather jacket.
  • Refinement, not search, is the heart of it: shoppers narrow down step by step, and the interface has to keep the state visible.
  • Building for voice and for agents at the same time is cheap when both go through one set of tools.

What's next

  • Run a live voice session and tune the prompt on real speech.
  • Swap the static snapshot for live search, and test a second category.
