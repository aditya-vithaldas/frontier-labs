Demo

Thesis

People happily ramble about what they want. Voice lets a store use all of it.

Problem

Search makes shoppers turn their needs into filters and keywords, and voice assistants have been too stop-start to hold a real conversation.

Solution

A live voice session runs over the whole store. Ask for laptops under a budget, explain that you game, and the results narrow as you talk. Voice tools search, refine, open products and explain terms, and they stay in sync with clicks and filters.
