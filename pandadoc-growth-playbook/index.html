Win the agent's choice, then earn the customer

How a document platform grows when the agent, not the person, picks the tool.

The question

Customers increasingly arrive like this: someone asks for a contract, the agent picks the tool, and that tool earns the customer. How do you grow in that world?

What we explored

Lever 1: be the agent's first choice

Agents choose by reading tool descriptions. Write them for the job (agreements, quotes, NDAs that need signing), not for the API. Metric: how often the agent picks you.

Lever 2: first agreements, no sign-up

Let the first one or two agreements go out on the agent's sign-in, and offer to claim an account once they are signed.

Levers 3 and 4: value before asking, pocket-sized plans

A finished draft before any form. Pay for the few agreements you need, then grow into seats.

What we found

  • The funnel now starts inside someone else's assistant. Tool descriptions are the new landing page.
  • Fuzzy template matching and failed sends that report success quietly lose the agent's trust.
  • Sign-up walls are friction an agent can't get past. Defer them.

What's next

  • Measure pick rate across assistants.
  • Pilot a no-sign-up first send.
